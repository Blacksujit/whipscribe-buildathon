Notes

What I changed and why:

  • Merged two header rows into one — saves ~30px of vertical space on a 320px screen. The original had "Keep reading" text above the title, which is redundant.
  • Replaced three panel openers (search, download, more) with one "Actions" button (⋯) — three different icons with different interaction patterns is confusing. One button opens a single bottom sheet with all three.
  • Trimmed the 9-item menu to 4 (Rename, Copy link, Details, Delete) — Settings, Timestamps, Quiz, Sign in, and Home are either not phone-relevant or belong elsewhere. "Quiz" is a desktop feature.
  • Added processing state — users currently see nothing while transcription runs. The new screen shows progress %, estimated time, speakers found so far, and lets the user listen to the partial audio.
  • Add multi-speaker support at 320px — all current screenshots are single-speaker. Meetings are a core use case. Color-coded dots + compact speaker labels fit without shrinking text below 12px.

What I deliberately kept: Timestamps in the gutter (essential for jumping to moments), the player at the bottom (industry standard for mobile media), and the free-preview "Keep reading" concept (kept as a subtle PREVIEW badge instead of a bottom bar that competes with the player).
