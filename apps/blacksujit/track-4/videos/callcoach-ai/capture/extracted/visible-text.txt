Your sales calls, scored. Your reps, coached. Your team, improved.

PRODUCT POSITIONING:
CallCoach-AI is an independent product — the AI quality layer for sales conversations. It integrates with WhipScribe (third-party transcription API) and adds a multi-agent LLM evaluation engine that scores every call across four dimensions.

THE PROBLEM:
Sales managers waste 5-10 hours per week reading call transcripts. WhipScribe gives them perfect audio-to-text, but the output is just words. Managers miss compliance risks (unbacked promises, missing disclosures), lose action items (the #1 cause of deal slippage), and can't track which reps are improving. Coaching is delayed by 2-3 days and inconsistent.

THE SOLUTION:
Four specialized LLM agents as judges — ComplianceAgent, TensionAgent, ClarityAgent, ActionItemAgent — each with a sales-quality rubric. Evidence-first scoring: every issue links to exact timestamp + quote. Trend intelligence across all calls.

KEY METRICS:
- 5-10 hours/week wasted on manual review
- 30-60 minutes per call to review
- Score range: 60-95 (realistic for actual calls)
- 4 quality dimensions: Action Items, Clarity, Tension, Compliance
- Zero missed action items with auto-extraction + owner/deadline tracking

DIFFERENTIATORS:
- Evidence-first scoring with clickable timestamps
- Multi-agent specialization (not one LLM prompt)
- Trend intelligence across all calls
- Built on real APIs: WhipScribe + GROQ
- No mocks, no fake data

ARCHITECTURE:
Call Recording → WhipScribe API (ASR + diarization) → 4-Agent LLM Pipeline (GROQ) → QA Report + Dashboard
