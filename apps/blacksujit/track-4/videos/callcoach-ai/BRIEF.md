## Intent

CallCoach-AI is the AI quality layer for sales conversations — an independent product that sits on top of WhipScribe's transcription API to deliver multi-agent LLM evaluation, scoring, and coaching intelligence. The video sells the problem and solution in 60 seconds: sales managers waste 5-10 hours/week reading transcripts, missing compliance risks, losing action items, and giving inconsistent feedback. CallCoach-AI stops them from watching recordings and makes them read a structured QA score with evidence timestamps. The tone is confident, professional, and data-forward — B2B SaaS, not stock-photo aesthetics.

## Assets

- README.md (track-4) — the product's positioning, tagline, and architecture; used as the source of truth for product copy and feature descriptions.
- No live website (no-capture mode). The video's visual identity comes from the CallCoach-AI brand: teal + white + dark slate, clean sans-serif typography, data-forward layout.
